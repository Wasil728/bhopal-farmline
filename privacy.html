LEGAL & DATA CHARTER

Privacy Policy

Edition: September 2026

01 — FOR DIRECTORY VISITORS

Visitor Privacy

Bhopal Farmline is an open-access community directory. We prioritize a minimal data footprint and do not monetize visitor information in any way:

  • We do not track personal behavioral data or build visitor profiles.
  • We do not require account creation or registration to browse listings.
  • We do not use invasive third-party advertising tracking pixels.
  • Direct calls and WhatsApp links connect you straight to the owner's phone — no intermediate interception or recording.
  • In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, we only collect the minimum data necessary to operate this directory and do not sell or share visitor data with third parties.
02 — FOR PROPERTY OWNERS

Owner Information

When you submit a listing, specific details become publicly accessible while others remain private:

  • Public Record: Farmhouse name, area, photographs, description, amenities, capacity, pricing, and the public inquiry phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Private Record: Your verification phone number (collected during submission for manual review) is kept confidential, is never displayed publicly, and is not shared with advertisers or third parties.
  • Data Retention: We retain your verification phone number only as long as your listing remains active. If a listing is removed or you request deletion, associated private verification data is deleted from our database.
  • Your Rights: In line with DPDPA principles, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your listing information at any time by contacting us at support.bhopalfarmline@gmail.com. We aim to action such requests within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Modification & Erasure: To update your listing details or remove it entirely, email our team at the address above.
03 — INFRASTRUCTURE & SECURITY

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Database (Supabase): Listing data, photos, and owner-submitted information is stored on Supabase, a managed PostgreSQL database provider. Supabase implements industry-standard security practices including encryption at rest and in transit. Data is stored on infrastructure hosted in secure cloud environments. You can review Supabase's own privacy and security documentation at supabase.com/security.
  • Content Delivery (jsDelivr): Some JavaScript libraries are loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which may collect standard anonymised web server access logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp). No personal information from our visitors is shared with jsDelivr.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (page views, general geography, device type). This does not collect personally identifiable information. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): Typography is served via Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of standard CDN delivery. No personal data from this site is shared with Google Fonts.
04 — YOUR RIGHTS UNDER DPDPA

Data Rights

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), along with the DPDP Rules, 2025, is being implemented in phases. As of September 2026, the Data Protection Board of India is established, and we operate our data practices in alignment with DPDPA principles as a matter of good practice — ahead of full mandatory compliance obligations which are expected to take effect through 2026–2027. We do not claim formal DPDPA certification or audit status.

As a data principal under this framework, you have the following rights in relation to any personal data you have shared with us (i.e. as a farmhouse owner who submitted a listing):

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of what personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can request correction of any inaccurate or outdated information in your listing.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your listing and associated personal data at any time.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: If you have a complaint or concern about how your data is handled, contact us at support.bhopalfarmline@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

As India's data protection framework (DPDPA 2023) continues its phased rollout through 2026 and 2027, we will update this policy to reflect new requirements as they become legally applicable.
