LEGAL & DATA CHARTER

Privacy Policy

Edition: August 2026

01 — FOR DIRECTORY VISITORS

Visitor Privacy

Bhopal Farmline is an open-access community directory. We prioritize minimal data footprint:

  • We do not track personal behavioral data or build user profiles.
  • We do not mandate user account creation or registration to browse.
  • We do not employ invasive third-party advertising tracking pixels.
  • Direct calls and WhatsApp links connect you straight to the owner's personal device — no intermediate chat interception.
02 — FOR PROPERTY OWNERS

Owner Information

Submitting a listing makes specific operational details accessible to the public:

  • Public Record: Estate title, regional belt, photographs, description, amenities, and public inquiry phone line.
  • Private Record: Owner verification telephone number is kept confidential and will never be shared with advertisers.
  • Modification & Erasure: To update details or remove a listing, contact our editorial team.
03 — INFRASTRUCTURE & SECURITY

Third-Party Services

Our infrastructure utilizes industry-standard partners:

  • Cloud Database: Secure backend storage adhering to international security standards.
  • Content Delivery: Fast global CDN edge caching collecting only standard anonymized web server access logs.
